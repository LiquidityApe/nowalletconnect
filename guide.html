How to Get Started

1
Get Your Merchant ID
Register and get your unique merchant ID (for example, NWC). Save your Polygon wallet, Solana wallet, and a return URL such as https://yoursite.com/order-complete. After payment, the buyer is sent back to that URL with success parameters.
2
Build a payment link
Send customers to the payment page with your merchant ID. Add amount and/or network only when you want those values locked. Anything you leave out, the customer fills in themselves.

Base URL:
https://solacediamond.github.io/nowalletconnect/pay.html?merchant=MERCHANT_ID
ParamRequiredIf presentIf missing
merchantYesShown on the pageLink is invalid
amountNoLocked. Customer cannot change itCustomer types the amount
networkNoLocked to polygon or solanaCustomer picks the network
Examples
Customer chooses amount and network:
pay.html?merchant=NWC

Fixed $49.99 package, customer picks network:
pay.html?merchant=NWC&amount=49.99

Fixed Solana, customer types amount:
pay.html?merchant=NWC&network=solana

Fully locked package:
pay.html?merchant=NWC&amount=49.99&network=polygon

Questions?

Can the customer change a locked amount?
No. If amount is in the URL, the field is read-only. If network is in the URL, those buttons are disabled.
How do I sell a specific package?
Put the package price in amount. Example: amount=9.99 for starter, amount=49.99 for pro. Use one button per package.
Where does the return URL come from?
From the Webhook URL saved on your merchant row. It is not passed in the payment link. Use a real page on your site.
How long does it take to set up?
About 5 minutes. Get a merchant ID, set wallets and a return URL, copy the button, add amount/network if you want them locked.
Do I need coding knowledge?
The Pay button is copy-paste. Reading the success return needs a few lines of JavaScript on your thank-you page.
Can I customize the Pay Now button?
Yes. Change the label, colors, size, or shape. Keep merchant in the href, add amount/network only when you want them locked, and keep the Powered by line.
What are the fees?
$1 setup when you sign up, plus $1/month for maintenance. No transaction fees. You keep 100% of every payment.
How do customers pay?
They open your link, fill any unlocked fields, send the exact USDT amount on the chosen network, and wait for automatic verification.
Where does the money go?
Directly to the wallets saved on your merchant account. No middleman and no extra cut from us.
